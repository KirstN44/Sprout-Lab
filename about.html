About Sprout Lab

Sprout Lab is an open educational portal built to provide clean, distraction-free practice tools for children. We focus on active cognitive practice across reading, math, and logical problem solving without ads, locked paywalls, or noisy gamification gimmicks.

Customizable Learning Content

Every learner develops along their own distinct timeline. Sprout Lab games are designed to accept custom configuration sets directly from teachers and parents:

  • Custom Word Lists: Input weekly classroom spelling lists and thematic vocabulary units directly into the Word Lab activities.
  • Sight Words Practice: Select specific high-frequency word groupings to emphasize rapid visual recognition.
  • Adaptive Math Problems: Tailor numerical ranges and arithmetic operations to reinforce current syllabus targets.

Whether you are preparing a small-group phonics station or providing supplemental practice at the kitchen table, Sprout Lab modules adapt to your curriculum needs.
